The Top 10 Players in Football Right Now

Ten stars. Ten different ways to take over a game. From game-breaking quarterbacks to pass rushers who wreck entire protections, these are the players sitting at the top of the NFL entering the heart of the 2026 season.

By Fourth & Long Staff • Updated September 25, 2026

This ranking follows the NFL’s 2026 player-voted Top 100 as its foundation. The order reflects how players across the league rated their peers, while the notes below keep the focus simple: what makes each star special right now.

10
CB • Denver Broncos

Patrick Surtain II

Surtain remains the standard for shutdown cornerback play. He rarely needs help, travels with elite receivers and gives Denver the freedom to be aggressive everywhere else on defense.

9
RB • Detroit Lions

Jahmyr Gibbs

Few backs create explosive plays as effortlessly as Gibbs. His burst makes him dangerous between the tackles, on the edge and as a receiver, turning ordinary touches into scoring chances.

8
EDGE • Green Bay Packers

Micah Parsons

When healthy, Parsons changes the math for an offense. His speed off the edge and ability to move across the front force protections to account for him before the ball is even snapped.

7
WR • Los Angeles Rams

Puka Nacua

Nacua has become one of football’s most complete receivers. He wins with strength, route feel and toughness after the catch, while his production has already placed him among the league’s most reliable offensive engines.

6
RB • San Francisco 49ers

Christian McCaffrey

McCaffrey remains the rare running back who can become the center of an entire offense. He can run inside, split wide, win as a receiver and punish mismatches before a defense knows where he will line up.

5
WR • Seattle Seahawks

Jaxon Smith-Njigba

Smith-Njigba’s leap into superstardom is complete. After leading the league in receiving yards in 2025, he enters 2026 as one of the NFL’s most difficult one-on-one covers and one of its most productive wideouts.

4
QB • Los Angeles Rams

Matthew Stafford

The reigning MVP is still carving defenses apart with anticipation, arm talent and command at the line. Stafford’s 2025 season proved that even deep into his career, he can still play quarterback at the sport’s highest level.

3
RB • Atlanta Falcons

Bijan Robinson

Robinson blends patience, power and receiving ability into a nearly positionless offensive weapon. His league-leading 2,298 scrimmage yards in 2025 made the case that no running back affects more areas of the field.

2
QB • Buffalo Bills

Josh Allen

Allen can beat a defense in almost every possible way. He has elite arm strength, creates outside structure and remains one of the most dangerous red-zone runners at the position.

1
EDGE • Los Angeles Rams

Myles Garrett

Garrett sits at No. 1 after a historic 23-sack season and another Defensive Player of the Year award. His power, speed and constant ability to draw extra protection make him the league’s ultimate defensive problem.

About the ranking

The order is based on the NFL Top 100 Players of 2026, a peer-voted ranking released by the league. The short descriptions on this page are original editorial summaries designed for a concise article-style presentation.
